1. Why Apparent Baker's Percentage Fails Above 15% Levain Inoculation

F_lev = M_lev / (1 + H_lev)  |  W_lev = M_lev - F_lev  |  True Total Hydration H_true = (W_added + W_lev) / (F_main + F_lev)

Naive Calculator Output (900g Flour, 75% Hydration, 180g Starter)

Multiplies 900g × 0.75 = 675.0g added water. Combined with 90g flour + 90g water inside the 100% starter, actual dough ratio is 765g water / 990g flour = 77.27% True Hydration (+22.5g excess water).

SourdoughCalc Reconciled Output (0.00% Error)

Decomposes starter mass first (990g Total Flour × 0.75 = 742.5g Total Water), then deducts 90g starter water to prescribe 652.5g added water—locking True Total Hydration at 75.00%.

Under AACC Method 54-21.02 Farinograph testing, that +2.27% uncounted starter water drops dough viscosity by 68 Brabender Units—turning a high-scoring country boule into a slack puddle.” — Julian Vance, Lead Cereal Rheology Engineer at SourdoughCalc

4. Solving Summer Kitchen Overheating via 80 cal/g Latent Heat of Fusion

In The Taste of Bread (Calvel, 2001), required water temperature is T_req = 4×DDT - (T_room + T_flour + T_levain + F_friction). When summer kitchens exceed 28°C (82°F), T_req drops below 0°C (32°F)—where liquid tap water cannot exist.

Enthalpy Balance (°C):   m_ice = [ W_total × (T_tap - T_req) ] / ( 79.72 + T_tap )   ≈   [ W_total × (T_tap - T_req) ] / ( 80 + T_tap )

26°C Warm Hand-Mix

W_total = 725g, T_tap = 22°C, T_req = 16°C.
Result: 42.6g Crushed Ice + 682.4g Tap Water locks DDT at 24.0°C (75.2°F).

28°C Summer Spiral Mix

W_total = 725g, T_tap = 24°C, T_req = +4°C.
Result: 139.4g Crushed Ice + 585.6g Tap Water locks DDT at 24.0°C.

31°C Heatwave Planetary

W_total = 725g, T_tap = 26°C, T_req = -7°C (19.4°F).
Result: 225.7g Crushed Ice + 499.3g Tap Water.

6. Starter Inoculation Kinetics: 1:1:1 vs. 1:5:5 vs. 1:10:10 Peak Curves

Per Gänzle (2014), heterofermentative Fructilactobacillus sanfranciscensis and wild yeasts follow a logarithmic growth curve with a Q₁₀ temperature coefficient of ~2.25 between 18°C and 28°C. Higher feeding ratios dilute organic acid carryover and extend peak dome windows:

1:1:1 Express Build

33.3% Inoculation: Peaks in 3.8 – 4.2 hrs at 25°C (77°F). High lactic/acetic carryover; ideal for same-day morning bakes.

1:5:5 Standard Overnight

9.1% Inoculation: Peaks in 8.5 – 9.5 hrs at 23°C (73.4°F). Optimal yeast-to-LAB balance for maximum oven spring.

1:10:10 Summer / High-Temp

4.8% Inoculation: Peaks in 11.0 – 12.5 hrs at 26°C (78.8°F). Prevents overnight starter collapse in warm kitchens.

7. Bidirectional Yeast ↔ Sourdough & Discard Acid Neutralization

Instant Dry Yeast (IDY) → Sourdough Levain

To convert a commercial yeast formula (F₀ flour, W₀ water) to starter mass M_s at hydration H_s with 0.0% hydration shift:

F_new = F₀ - M_s / (1 + H_s)
W_new = W₀ - (M_s × H_s) / (1 + H_s)

Also converts between IDY (1.0×), Active Dry Yeast (1.33×), and Fresh Cake Yeast (3.0×).

Zero-Waste Discard Substitution & NaHCO₃ Buffering

Every 100g of unfed 100% hydration discard replaces 50g flour + 50g liquid in pancakes, waffles, muffins, or crackers.

To neutralize accumulated lactic/acetic acid (pH 3.9–4.2) without soapy off-flavors, buffer with 1.2g – 1.5g sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃) per 100g discard.
