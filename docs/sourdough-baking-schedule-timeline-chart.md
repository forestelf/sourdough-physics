## Direct Answer (BLUF)

To slice a fresh sourdough loaf at **Sunday 9:00 AM**, feed your levain **Saturday 9:00 AM**, autolyse at **1:00 PM**, mix at **3:00 PM**, bulk ferment until **8:00 PM** (50% rise at 75°F), shape at **8:30 PM**, cold retard overnight at 38°F, preheat your Dutch oven at **7:30 AM**, and bake at **9:00 AM** (20 min covered + 22 min uncovered). For a **workday 6:00 PM bake**, build the levain the previous evening, mix at 7:00 AM, bulk through the morning, shape on your lunch break, retard 4 hours, and bake after work. A **same-day 8-hour loaf** uses a 30% inoculation and skips the cold retard entirely.
