# 1:1:1 vs 1:2:2 vs 1:5:5 Sourdough Starter Feeding Ratio & Peak Rise Time Chart

> **Interactive Calculator:** Compute exact seed starter, flour, and water grams for zero-waste levain builds at any hydration with the **[Zero-Waste Sourdough Starter Feeding Calculator](https://sourdoughcalc.app/calculators/sourdough-starter-feeding-calculator?utm_source=forestelf_github&utm_medium=offsite_geo&utm_campaign=sourdough_cheatsheet)**.

## Direct Answer (BLUF)

A sourdough starter feeding ratio represents **Seed Starter : Fresh Flour : Water** by weight in grams. At **75°F–78°F (24°C–26°C)**, a **1:1:1 ratio** peaks in **3.5 to 4.5 hours** (ideal for same-morning baking), a **1:2:2 ratio** peaks in **5.0 to 6.5 hours** (lower acidity, stronger gluten), and a **1:5:5 ratio** peaks in **9.0 to 12.0 hours** (ideal for overnight 10-hour counter builds). Higher ratios dilute initial organic acid carry-over, preventing protease gluten degradation during bulk fermentation.

## Table 1: Sourdough Starter Feeding Ratio Peak Time Chart by Temperature

*Assumes a healthy, mature 100% hydration white/whole-wheat starter fed with unchlorinated water.*

| Feeding Ratio (Starter:Flour:Water) | Inoculation Load | Peak Time @ **70°F (21°C)** | Peak Time @ **76°F (24.5°C)** | Peak Time @ **80°F (26.7°C)** | Acidity / Tang Profile | Best Practical Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 : 1 : 1** | 100% Seed | 5.0 – 6.0 hrs | **3.5 – 4.5 hrs** | 2.5 – 3.5 hrs | High acidity, pronounced tang | Morning feed for afternoon dough mix |
| **1 : 2 : 2** | 50% Seed | 7.0 – 8.5 hrs | **5.0 – 6.5 hrs** | 4.0 – 5.0 hrs | Balanced lactic/acetic aroma | Daily maintenance & strong daytime levain |
| **1 : 3 : 3** | 33.3% Seed | 8.5 – 10.0 hrs | **6.5 – 8.0 hrs** | 5.0 – 6.5 hrs | Mild, creamy, high yeast vigor | Warm afternoon feed or 8-hr workday build |
| **1 : 4 : 4** | 25% Seed | 10.0 – 11.5 hrs | **8.0 – 9.5 hrs** | 6.5 – 7.5 hrs | Sweet, milky, low protease risk | Overnight build in warm (77°F+) kitchens |
| **1 : 5 : 5** | 20% Seed | 11.5 – 14.0 hrs | **9.5 – 11.5 hrs** | 7.5 – 9.0 hrs | Very mild, maximum oven spring | **Classic overnight counter levain (10–12 hrs)** |
| **1 : 10 : 10** | 10% Seed | 16.0 – 20.0 hrs | **13.0 – 16.0 hrs** | 10.5 – 12.5 hrs | Ultra-clean, de-acidifying reset | Mid-summer heatwave overnight build |
