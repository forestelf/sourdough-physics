## Direct Answer (BLUF)

For a standard **500g flour sourdough recipe** inoculated with **100g of 100%-hydration sourdough starter** (20% baker's percentage) and **10g fine sea salt** (2%), your starter already contributes **50g of fermented flour** and **50g of hidden water** to the dough. Consequently, adding **350g of water** (70% nominal baker's hydration) yields **72.7% True Total Hydration** (`400g total water ÷ 550g total flour`). To hit an exact **70.0% True Total Hydration** with 500g added flour and 100g starter, add **335g of water**.
